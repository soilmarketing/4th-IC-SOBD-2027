About the conference

Artificial Intelligence has emerged as one of the most transformative technologies of the twenty-first century, reshaping industries, economies, governance systems and everyday life. From intelligent automation and predictive analytics to generative AI and autonomous decision-making, it is revolutionising how organisations create value, deliver services and address complex societal challenges.

At the same time, the growing urgency of climate change, resource scarcity, social inequality, ethical governance and sustainable development calls for responsible technological innovation that balances economic growth with environmental stewardship and human well-being.

This conference seeks to provide a global platform to deliberate upon the opportunities, challenges and future directions of AI-driven sustainable transformation, encouraging interdisciplinary dialogue that bridges technology, management, public policy, economics, social sciences, design thinking and sustainability studies.

It aligns with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, India's National AI Mission, Industry 5.0 principles, ESG frameworks and responsible innovation initiatives, exploring how AI can contribute towards building resilient organisations, inclusive economies, ethical governance, smart cities, sustainable industries and future-ready societies.

Conference objectives

  • Foster interdisciplinary research on AI-driven sustainable development
  • Promote responsible, ethical and human-centric AI
  • Explore AI applications across business, governance and society
  • Encourage academia and industry collaboration
  • Showcase innovative research and emerging technologies
  • Discuss policy implications of AI for sustainable economic development
  • Facilitate global networking among researchers and practitioners

How to submit

Submissions must be original and unpublished. Every submission goes through double-blind peer review.

Extended abstract

  • Length 800 to 1,000 words
  • Originality maximum similarity 5 per cent, and no AI-generated content
  • Title clear and concise
  • Author details names, affiliations, emails and the corresponding author
  • Introduction context, relevance and the problem statement
  • Objectives and research questions what the study aims to address
  • Methodology a brief description of design, data or approach
  • Key findings or expected outcomes main insights or anticipated results
  • Implications theoretical, managerial or policy relevance
  • Keywords four to six
  • References up to 15, in a standard style such as APA

Full paper

  • Length 4,000 to 6,000 words
  • Typeface Times New Roman, 12 point
  • Line spacing 1.5
  • Referencing APA 7th edition
  • Review double-blind peer review
We welcome empirical research papers, conceptual papers, case studies, industry papers and review papers.
